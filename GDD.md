# Game Design Document

This game is about switching between characters, while hordes of enemies are attacking you.

## Genre

Idle-Platformer

## Primary Mechanic

### Switch

You have a random selection of 3 characters available with different movesets.
After using a character the slot gets replaced by a different random character.
So you have to plan which character to use now and which to save up.
Switching has a cooldown.

### Power Move

Replaying the character that you currently play as makes you perform a Power Move.
This temporarily increases your stats or performs a single move with more damage.

## Levels

The player character automatically progresses to the right and scrolls through the level.
The level defines fix enemy encounters after scrolling past a certain point.
Each section also defines a certain spawn rate by time.

## Goal 

The goal is to get to the end of a level.

## Characters

There are several characters to switch between.

### Knight

The moves slowly and attacks slowly with medium AOE attacks.
He will mostly stall enemies, while tanking many attacks.

Its Power Move performs a wide area medium damage attack, killing masses of smaller enemies.

### Wizard

The constantly attacks on long range with light big AOE spells (targeting flying enemies).
His weakness is sturdy enemies.

His Power Move teleports him forward.

### Samurai

The samurai deals medium range high damage.

As a Power move he may restore a flat amount of health.

### Shinobi

The shinobi deals fast close range damage.
Moving fast.

For his power move he jumps to the biggest enemy and deals massive single target damage.

### Vampire

The vampire is the weakest in pure damage, dealing medium damage on single target close range.
Every recovers hitpoints though, so use if few enemies are around to heal back up.

The power move lets him siphon a bit of HP from all enemies and giving an move and attack boost for a short time.

## Enemies

### Ghost

Low HP fast flying meele.

### Mintaur

Sturdy slow meele with defenses and high damage.

### Satyr

Heals other enemies.

### Skeleton

Canonfodder, low damage and HP.

### Werewolf

Fast self healing sturdy.
